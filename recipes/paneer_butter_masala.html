Paneer Butter Masala

Description of Paneer Butter Masala 🥘

Paneer Butter Masala is a popular and delicious North Indian vegetarian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich, creamy, and flavorful tomato-based gravy. The gravy is prepared with butter, tomatoes, onions, ginger, garlic, and a blend of aromatic Indian spices such as garam masala, coriander powder, and red chili powder. A touch of cream adds a smooth and rich texture to the curry, while butter gives it a delicious taste and aroma.

The combination of soft paneer and mildly spicy, creamy gravy makes Paneer Butter Masala a perfect dish for lunch, dinner, or special occasions. It is commonly served hot with naan, roti, chapati, paratha, or steamed basmati rice. Fresh coriander and a little cream can be added on top to make the dish even more attractive and flavorful.

Ingredients of Paneer Butter Masala 🥘

To prepare a delicious and restaurant-style Paneer Butter Masala, you’ll need a combination of soft paneer, fresh tomatoes, butter, cream, aromatic spices, and a few basic ingredients. Tomatoes provide a rich and slightly tangy base, while butter and fresh cream give the gravy its smooth, creamy texture. Ginger, garlic, onions, cashews, and aromatic spices add depth and a wonderful flavor to the curry. All these ingredients come together to create a rich, creamy, mildly spicy, and flavorful Paneer Butter Masala that pairs perfectly with naan, roti, paratha, or steamed rice.

Main Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 g, cut into medium-sized cubes
  • Green peas (Matar) – 1 cup
  • Onion – 1 medium, roughly chopped
  • Tomatoes – 3–4 medium, finely chopped or pureed
  • Ginger– 1-inch piece, finely chopped
  • Garlic– 4–5 cloves, minced
  • Green chili– 1, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander leaves– 2 tablespoons, chopped

Spices

  • Cumin seeds (Jeera)– 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Red chili powder– 1 teaspoon
  • Coriander powder– 1 teaspoons
  • Garam masala– ½ teaspoon
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)– 1 teaspoon
  • Salt– according to taste

Other Ingredients

  • Cooking oil or ghee– 1 tablespoons
  • Water– 1–1½ cups, as needed
  • Cashews– 10–12 (for a rich and creamy gravy)
  • Fresh cream– 2-3 tablespoons (optional, for a creamy gravy)
  • Butter– 2-3 tablespoon (optional)

Step-by-Step Guide to Making Perfect Paneer Butter Masala 🥘

Follow these detailed steps to prepare a rich, creamy, and flavorful Paneer Butter Masala at home. The key to making a delicious curry is to prepare a smooth gravy, cook the spices properly, and allow the paneer to absorb all the wonderful flavors.

  1. Prepare the Paneer - Start by cutting the paneer into medium-sized cubes. Try to keep the pieces roughly the same size so that they cook evenly. Heat a little oil or butter in a pan over medium heat and lightly fry the paneer cubes until they become slightly golden on the outside.
    Do not fry the paneer for too long because over-frying can make it firm. Once lightly golden, remove the paneer from the pan and keep it aside. You can also place the fried paneer in warm water for a few minutes to help keep it soft.
  2. Prepare the Tomato and Cashew Mixture - Take a separate pan and add the chopped tomatoes, cashews, ginger, garlic, and green chili. Add a little water and cook the mixture over medium heat until the tomatoes become completely soft and the cashews are tender.
    Turn off the heat and allow the mixture to cool slightly. Once cooled, transfer it to a blender and blend until you get a smooth and creamy puree. If necessary, add a little water while blending to achieve a smooth consistency.
  3. Strain the Gravy - For a smooth, restaurant-style Paneer Butter Masala, you can strain the blended tomato mixture through a fine sieve. This removes tomato seeds and any remaining pieces of skin or cashew, giving the gravy a silky texture.
    This step is optional, but it can make the final curry much smoother and more attractive.
  4. Prepare the Butter Base - Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a clean pan over low to medium heat. You can add a small amount of oil along with the butter to prevent the butter from burning.
    Add cumin seeds and allow them to crackle for a few seconds. Then add finely chopped onions and sauté them until they become soft and light golden. Stir occasionally so that the onions cook evenly.
  5. Add the Tomato Puree - Once the onions are cooked, carefully add the prepared tomato and cashew puree to the pan. Mix everything thoroughly with the butter and onions.
    Cook the mixture over medium heat for several minutes, stirring occasionally. Allow the excess moisture to cook away and continue until the gravy becomes thicker and the butter begins to appear around the edges.
  6. Add the Spices - Now add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt according to your taste. Mix the spices thoroughly into the gravy.
    Cook the mixture for a few minutes so that the spices release their aroma and blend properly with the tomato base. Avoid cooking the spices on very high heat, as they can burn and make the curry bitter.
  7. Adjust the Gravy Consistency - Add approximately 1–1½ cups of water, depending on how thick you want your curry to be. Mix everything well and allow the gravy to simmer over low to medium heat.
    Letting the gravy simmer helps the spices blend together and creates a richer flavor. If you prefer a thicker restaurant-style curry, use less water and simmer it for a little longer.
  8. Add the Paneer Cubes - Gently add the prepared paneer cubes to the gravy. Mix carefully so that the paneer pieces remain whole and do not break.
    Cover the pan and let the curry simmer on low heat for about 5–7 minutes. During this time, the paneer will absorb the flavors of the creamy tomato gravy and become more flavorful.
  9. Add Cream, Garam Masala and Kasuri Methi - Now add fresh cream, garam masala, and crushed kasuri methi. Stir gently until everything is well combined.
    The cream gives the curry its characteristic smooth and rich texture, while garam masala and kasuri methi add a wonderful aroma and depth of flavor. Cook for another 1–2 minutes on low heat.
  10. Add the Final Touch of Butter - For an extra rich and flavorful curry, add a small amount of butter at the end. Stir gently and allow it to melt into the gravy.
    Taste the curry and adjust the salt or spices if needed. If the gravy is too thick, add a little warm water and mix until you reach your preferred consistency.
  11. Garnish the Paneer Butter Masala - Turn off the heat and transfer the curry into a serving bowl. Garnish it with freshly chopped coriander leaves and a small swirl of fresh cream.
    You can also add a tiny piece of butter on top while the curry is hot. This gives the dish an attractive restaurant-style appearance and adds extra richness.
  12. Serve and Enjoy - Your delicious Paneer Butter Masala is now ready to serve! Serve it hot with butter naan, garlic naan, roti, chapati, paratha, or steamed basmati rice.
    The combination of soft paneer cubes, creamy tomato-cashew gravy, aromatic spices, butter, and fresh cream makes this dish rich, comforting, and perfect for a special lunch or dinner. 🥘🧈✨
