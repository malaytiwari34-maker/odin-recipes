Shahi Paneer

Description of Shahi Paneer🥘

Shahi Paneer is a rich, creamy, and delicious North Indian vegetarian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a smooth and flavorful gravy. The word “Shahi” means royal, and this dish is known for its rich taste and luxurious texture. The gravy is usually prepared with tomatoes, onions, cashews, cream, ginger, garlic, and a blend of aromatic Indian spices.

The soft paneer absorbs the delicious flavors of the creamy gravy, while cashews and fresh cream give the curry a smooth and rich texture. Mild spices add a wonderful aroma without making the dish overly spicy. Shahi Paneer is often prepared for special occasions, family gatherings, festivals, and celebrations. It tastes especially delicious when served hot with naan, roti, paratha, chapati, or fragrant basmati rice. 🥘✨

Ingredients of Shahi Paneer 🥘

To prepare a delicious and royal-style Shahi Paneer, you’ll need a combination of soft paneer, fresh tomatoes, onions, cashews, cream, butter, and aromatic Indian spices. Cashews and cream give the gravy its rich and smooth texture, while tomatoes and onions provide a flavorful base. Ginger, garlic, green chilies, cardamom, cinnamon, and garam masala add a wonderful aroma and depth to the curry. All these ingredients come together to create a rich, creamy, mildly spiced, and royal-tasting Shahi Paneer that pairs perfectly with naan, roti, paratha, chapati, or steamed basmati rice.

Main Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 g, cut into medium-sized cubes
  • Cashews – 12-15
  • Onion – 2 medium, finely chopped
  • Tomatoes – 2 medium, finely chopped or pureed
  • Ginger– 1-inch piece, finely chopped
  • Garlic– 4–5 cloves, minced
  • Green chili– 1, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander leaves– 2 tablespoons, chopped

Spices

  • Cumin seeds (Jeera)– 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Red chili powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander powder– 1 teaspoons
  • Garam masala– ½ teaspoon
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)– 1 teaspoon
  • Salt– according to taste

Other Ingredients

  • Cooking oil or ghee– 1 tablespoons
  • Water– 1–1½ cups, as needed
  • Fresh cream– 3 tablespoons (optional, for a creamy gravy)
  • Butter– 2 tablespoon (optional)
  • Milk - ½ cup (optional)
  • Cardamom– 2–3 pods
  • Cinnamon– 1 small piece
  • Bay leaf– 1

Step-by-Step Guide to Making Perfect Shahi Paneer

Follow these detailed steps to prepare a rich, creamy, and royal Shahi Paneer at home. The key to making a perfect curry is to prepare a smooth and flavorful cashew-based gravy, cook the spices properly, and allow the soft paneer cubes to absorb all the delicious flavors.

  1. Prepare the Paneer - Start by cutting the paneer into medium-sized cubes. Make sure the cubes are similar in size so they cook evenly. Heat a small amount of oil or butter in a pan and lightly fry the paneer cubes until they become slightly golden on the outside. Avoid frying them for too long, as this can make the paneer hard. Once done, remove the paneer and keep it aside.
  2. Prepare the Cashew Mixture - Take a separate pan and add the chopped onions, tomatoes, cashews, ginger, garlic, and green chili. Add a little water and cook everything over medium heat until the onions and tomatoes become soft and the cashews are tender.
    Turn off the heat and allow the mixture to cool slightly. Transfer it to a blender and blend until you get a smooth and creamy paste.
  3. Prepare the Aromatic Spices - Heat 1–2 tablespoons of butter in a clean pan over low to medium heat. Add cumin seeds, cardamom, cinnamon, and bay leaf. Allow them to cook for a few seconds until they release their pleasant aroma.
    Be careful not to burn the spices, as this can make the gravy bitter.
  4. Cook the Gravy - Add the prepared onion, tomato, and cashew paste to the pan. Mix everything well with the butter and aromatic spices. Cook the mixture over medium heat for several minutes, stirring occasionally.
    Continue cooking until the gravy becomes thick and the raw smell of the ingredients disappears.
  5. Add the Spices - Now add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt according to your taste. Mix the spices thoroughly into the gravy and cook for another few minutes.
    Cooking the spices properly helps them release their aroma and blend beautifully with the creamy gravy.
  6. Adjust the GravyAdd approximately ½–1 cup of water or milk to the gravy, depending on your preferred consistency. Mix well and allow the gravy to simmer on low heat for a few minutes.
    For a smoother and richer Shahi Paneer, you can add a little more milk or cream at this stage.
  7. Add the Paneer - Gently add the fried paneer cubes to the prepared gravy. Mix carefully so that the paneer cubes remain intact.
    Cover the pan and allow the curry to simmer on low heat for about 5–7 minutes. This allows the paneer to absorb the rich flavors of the gravy.
  8. Add Cream and Final Spices - Add 2–3 tablespoons of fresh cream, followed by garam masala and crushed kasuri methi. Stir everything gently until well combined.
    Cook for another 1–2 minutes on low heat. Avoid boiling the curry vigorously after adding the cream.
  9. Garnish the Shahi Paneer - Turn off the heat and transfer the Shahi Paneer to a serving bowl. Garnish it with freshly chopped coriander leaves and a small swirl of fresh cream.
    You can also sprinkle a few finely chopped cashews on top for an attractive presentation and extra crunch.
  10. Serve and Enjoy - Your rich and delicious Shahi Paneer is now ready to serve! Serve it hot with naan, roti, paratha, chapati, or steamed basmati rice.
    The combination of soft paneer, creamy cashew gravy, aromatic spices, and fresh cream makes Shahi Paneer a perfect dish for special occasions, family dinners, celebrations, and festive meals. 🥘✨
