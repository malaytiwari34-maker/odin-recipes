Palak Paneer

Description of Palak Paneer 🥬

Palak Paneer is a popular and delicious North Indian vegetarian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a rich and flavorful spinach-based gravy. Fresh spinach is blended into a smooth, vibrant green puree and cooked with onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, green chilies, and aromatic Indian spices. The soft paneer cubes absorb the flavors of the creamy spinach gravy, giving the dish a delicious combination of freshness, mild spice, and richness.

Palak Paneer is a comforting and nutritious meal that can be enjoyed for lunch or dinner. It is commonly served hot with roti, naan, chapati, paratha, or steamed rice. Fresh cream or a little butter can also be added to make the gravy richer and creamier.

Ingredients of Palak Paneer 🥬

To prepare a delicious and flavorful Palak Paneer, you’ll need a combination of fresh spinach, soft paneer, aromatic vegetables, and traditional Indian spices. Fresh spinach gives the curry its beautiful green color and refreshing flavor, while paneer adds a soft and creamy texture. Onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chilies create a flavorful base, and spices such as cumin, turmeric, coriander, and garam masala add a delicious aroma and taste. Together, these simple ingredients make a rich, creamy, and satisfying Palak Paneer that can be enjoyed with roti, naan, paratha, or rice.

Main Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 g, cut into cubes
  • Fresh spinach (Palak) – 500 g
  • Onion – 1 large, finely chopped
  • Tomatoes – 2 medium, finely chopped
  • Ginger– 1-inch piece, finely chopped
  • Garlic– 4–5 cloves, minced
  • Green chili– 1–2, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander leaves– 2 tablespoons, chopped

Spices

  • Cumin seeds (Jeera)– 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Red chili powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander powder– 1 teaspoons
  • Garam masala– ½ teaspoon
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)– 1 teaspoon
  • Salt– according to taste

Other Ingredients

  • Cooking oil – 2–3 tablespoons
  • Water– 1–1½ cups, as needed
  • Fresh cream– 2 tablespoons (optional)
  • Butter– 1 tablespoon (optional)

Step-by-Step Guide to Making Perfect Palak Paneer 🥬

Follow these detailed steps to prepare a creamy, flavorful, and delicious Palak Paneer at home. The key to making a perfect curry is to prepare fresh and smooth spinach puree, cook the spices properly, and allow the soft paneer cubes to absorb the rich flavors of the spinach gravy.

  1. Prepare the Spinach - Start by thoroughly washing the fresh spinach leaves under running water to remove any dirt or impurities. Remove the thick stems if needed and keep the clean leaves aside. Bring a pot of water to a boil and add the spinach leaves. Blanch the spinach for about 2–3 minutes until the leaves become soft and bright green.
    Immediately transfer the blanched spinach to a bowl of cold water. This helps maintain its vibrant green color. After a few minutes, drain the spinach and blend it into a smooth puree using a blender. Keep the spinach puree aside for later.
  2. Prepare the Paneer - Cut the paneer into medium-sized cubes. Heat 1–2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the paneer cubes and lightly fry them until they become slightly golden on the outside.
    Do not over-fry the paneer because it can become hard and chewy. Once the paneer is lightly golden, remove the cubes from the pan and keep them aside. You can also place them in warm water for a few minutes to help keep them soft.
  3. Prepare the Onion and Spice Base - In the same pan, add a little more oil if required. Add cumin seeds and allow them to crackle for a few seconds. Then add finely chopped onions and sauté them until they become soft and light golden.
    Add finely chopped ginger, garlic, and green chilies. Cook everything for about 1–2 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the raw smell disappears and the mixture becomes aromatic.
  4. Add Tomatoes and Spices - Add the chopped or pureed tomatoes to the pan and mix them well with the onion mixture. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt according to your taste.
    Cook the mixture over medium heat until the tomatoes become soft and the spices are properly combined. Continue cooking until the mixture becomes thick and the oil begins to separate from the sides of the pan. This creates a flavorful base for the spinach gravy.
  5. Add the Spinach Puree - Now add the prepared spinach puree to the cooked onion and tomato mixture. Stir everything together until the puree is evenly combined with the spices.
    Cook the spinach gravy over low to medium heat for about 5–7 minutes. Avoid cooking it for too long, as this can affect the fresh green color of the spinach. If the gravy is too thick, add a little water and adjust the consistency according to your preference.
  6. Add the Paneer - Once the spinach gravy is cooked and reaches your desired consistency, gently add the fried paneer cubes. Mix them carefully so that the paneer remains intact and does not break.
    Allow the paneer to simmer in the spinach gravy for about 5 minutes. This gives the paneer enough time to absorb the delicious flavors of the spinach and spices.
  7. Add Garam Masala and Kasuri Methi - Add ½ teaspoon of garam masala and crushed kasuri methi to the curry. Mix gently and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
    For a richer and creamier Palak Paneer, you can add 1–2 tablespoons of fresh cream or a small amount of butter. Mix well and let the curry cook for another minute.
  8. Check the Consistency and Taste - Taste the curry and adjust the salt or spices if necessary. If the gravy is too thick, add a small amount of water and mix well. If it is too thin, allow it to simmer for a few more minutes until it reaches the desired consistency.
    The final gravy should be smooth, flavorful, and thick enough to coat the paneer cubes nicely.
  9. Garnish the Palak Paneer - Turn off the heat and transfer the Palak Paneer to a serving bowl. Garnish it with freshly chopped coriander leaves. You can also add a small swirl of fresh cream or a little butter on top to make it look more attractive and rich.
  10. Serve and Enjoy - Your delicious Palak Paneer is now ready to serve! Serve it hot with roti, naan, chapati, paratha, or steamed basmati rice. The combination of creamy spinach gravy and soft paneer makes this dish a perfect choice for a satisfying lunch or dinner. 🥬🧀🍚
