Matar Paneer

Description of Matar Paneer

Matar Paneer is a classic and flavorful North Indian vegetarian dish that combines soft, juicy paneer cubes with fresh green peas in a deliciously spiced gravy. The gravy is prepared using tomatoes, onions, ginger, garlic, and a blend of aromatic Indian spices such as cumin, turmeric, coriander powder, and garam masala. The paneer absorbs the rich flavors of the gravy while the green peas add a natural sweetness and pleasant texture to the dish.

This comforting curry is easy to prepare and is enjoyed by people of all ages. It can be made for everyday meals as well as special occasions, family gatherings, and festive dinners. Matar Paneer tastes especially delicious when served hot with roti, naan, paratha, chapati, or steamed basmati rice. A little fresh coriander on top adds color, freshness, and extra flavor, making Matar Paneer a satisfying and wholesome Indian meal.

Ingredients of Matar Paneer

To make a delicious and flavorful Matar Paneer, you’ll need a combination of fresh vegetables, soft paneer, green peas, aromatic spices, and a few basic cooking ingredients. Fresh onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chilies create a rich and flavorful base, while spices such as cumin, turmeric, coriander powder, and garam masala add a wonderful Indian taste and aroma. These simple ingredients come together to make a rich, comforting, and satisfying curry that can be enjoyed with roti, naan, paratha, or rice.

Main Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 g, cut into cubes
  • Green peas (Matar) – 1 cup
  • Onion – 1 large, finely chopped
  • Tomatoes – 2–3 medium, finely chopped or pureed
  • Ginger– 1-inch piece, finely chopped
  • Garlic– 4–5 cloves, minced
  • Green chili– 1–2, finely chopped
  • Fresh coriander leaves– 2 tablespoons, chopped

Spices

  • Cumin seeds (Jeera)– 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder– ½ teaspoon
  • Red chili powder– 1 teaspoon
  • Coriander powder– 1½ teaspoons
  • Garam masala– ½–1 teaspoon
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)– 1 teaspoon
  • Salt– according to taste

Other Ingredients

  • Cooking oil or ghee– 2–3 tablespoons
  • Water– 1–1½ cups, as needed
  • Fresh cream– 2 tablespoons (optional, for a creamy gravy)
  • Butter– 1 tablespoon (optional)

Step-by-Step Guide to Making Perfect Matar Paneer

Follow these detailed steps to prepare a delicious, flavorful, and comforting Matar Paneer at home. The key to making a perfect curry is to prepare a rich and aromatic gravy, cook the spices properly, and allow the soft paneer and green peas to absorb all the wonderful flavors.

  1. Prepare All the Ingredients - Start by preparing all the ingredients before you begin cooking. Cut the paneer into medium-sized cubes so that they are easy to cook and eat. Finely chop the onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chilies. If you are using fresh green peas, remove them from their pods and wash them properly. Keep all the spices measured and ready so that they can be added easily while cooking.
  2. Lightly Fry the Paneer - Heat 1–2 tablespoons of cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat. Once the oil is hot, carefully add the paneer cubes and fry them lightly on all sides until they turn a beautiful golden color. Avoid frying the paneer for too long, as it can become hard. Once done, remove the paneer from the pan and place it on a plate. You can also keep the fried paneer in warm water for a few minutes to keep it soft.
  3. Prepare the Base for the Gravy - Use the same pan to prepare the curry so that the gravy gets the delicious flavor left behind by the paneer. Add a little more oil if required and heat it over medium flame. Add cumin seeds and allow them to crackle for a few seconds. Then add the finely chopped onions and sauté them until they become soft and light golden brown. Stir occasionally to prevent the onions from burning.
  4. Add Ginger, Garlic and Green Chilies - Once the onions are cooked, add finely chopped or crushed ginger and garlic along with the green chilies. Mix everything well and cook for about 1–2 minutes. Continue stirring until the raw smell of the ginger and garlic disappears and the mixture becomes fragrant. This step gives the curry a delicious aroma and adds depth to its flavor.
  5. Cook the Tomatoes and Spices - Now add the chopped or pureed tomatoes to the pan and mix them well with the onion mixture. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt according to your taste. Cook the mixture over medium heat until the tomatoes become soft and the spices are well combined. Continue cooking until the mixture becomes thick and the oil starts to separate from the sides. This indicates that the gravy base is properly cooked.
  6. Add the Green Peas - Add the green peas to the prepared tomato gravy and mix them thoroughly so that they are coated with the spices. Pour approximately 1 cup of water into the pan, depending on how thick you want the gravy to be. Cover the pan with a lid and allow the peas to cook until they become tender. Stir occasionally and add a little more water if the gravy becomes too thick.
  7. Add the Paneer Cubes - Once the green peas are cooked, gently add the fried paneer cubes to the gravy. Mix carefully so that the paneer cubes do not break. Allow the curry to simmer on low heat for about 5–7 minutes. This allows the paneer to absorb the delicious flavors of the tomato-based gravy and spices.
  8. Add Garam Masala and Kasuri Methi - After the paneer has absorbed the flavors, add garam masala and crushed kasuri methi to the curry. Mix everything gently and cook for another 1–2 minutes. If you prefer a richer and creamier Matar Paneer, you can add a small amount of fresh cream at this stage and mix it well with the gravy.
  9. Garnish the Matar Paneer - Turn off the heat once the curry reaches your desired consistency. Sprinkle freshly chopped coriander leaves over the top. The fresh coriander adds a pleasant aroma, color, and freshness to the finished dish. Allow the curry to rest for a couple of minutes before serving.
  10. Serve and Enjoy - Your delicious homemade Matar Paneer is now ready to serve! Transfer it to a serving bowl and garnish with a little more fresh coriander. Serve it hot with roti, naan, chapati, paratha, or steamed basmati rice. The combination of soft paneer, sweet green peas, and flavorful gravy makes this dish a perfect choice for lunch or dinner. 🥘🍚
